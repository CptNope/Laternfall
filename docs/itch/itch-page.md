## Description (paste into the description editor)

**The Lanterns are going out.**

Long ago, the Hollow Night swallowed the world. Four Lanterns were lit to chain it beneath the earth — Ember, Tide, Stone and Dawn. For a thousand years their flames held. …Until tonight.

You are Arin, the young lamplighter of Brightwater. When the Ember Lantern goes dark, you and your oldest friend Lyra set out to rekindle it. A hooded woman is snuffing the Lanterns one by one, and her voice is one Lyra hasn't heard in seven years.

**Lanternfall is a love letter to classic 16-bit JRPGs.** Every character, monster, town, dungeon and spell effect is drawn as vector graphics in code, and the whole chiptune soundtrack is composed and played live in your browser. There are no image or audio files at all.

### Features
- **5 playable heroes**: a lamplighter, a flamecaller, a tide priest, a rogue, and a duskmage with a story of her own
- **Classic turn-based battles** with magic, elemental weaknesses and absorbs, rare steals, status effects, bosses that change tactics when hurt, and **12 combo techs** where two or three heroes attack together
- **A big world to explore** on foot, by ship, by airship, by diving bell and on lanternmoths: 9 towns, 12 dungeons (32 floors), a desert continent, a valley you can only reach by air, and a whole sea floor with a sunken city
- **Chapter VI: The Crownless King**: a post-game story on an island that rises from the sea after the credits
- **Hearts of the Lamplighters**: a personal quest for every hero, each ending in a **Limit** super move and an ultimate weapon
- **Walk inside every building**: shopkeepers behind counters, inns, homes and a palace, plus barrels and pots to search for hidden treasure
- **94 monsters** and **17 boss battles**, including two two-phase finales, a sea monster, a forgotten king, an optional superboss dragon, a solo duel, a drowned king and a secret superboss
- **Fishing** (13 fish) and **forging** (12 recipes from monster materials)
- **The Endless Lantern**: a dungeon with no bottom for after the ending, and **Hollow mode** for a hard run with no healing at inns
- **A living world**: day and night with night-only monsters and people, and regional weather that changes battles
- ***Lanterns & Lies***, a card game against eight players, and a night **heist** past patrolling guards
- **A home of your own**: upgrade Arin's house with a fish tank, a trophy shelf and a music box that plays every song you've heard, and let Mittens the cat follow you around town
- **23 side quests**, buried **treasure maps**, a **6-cup Arena**, a **bestiary**, a **world map**, dozens of accessories, a **Lantern Board** for every hero, **cooking**, and **New Game+**
- **Play anywhere**: in the browser, installed as an offline app from [cptnope.github.io/Laternfall](https://cptnope.github.io/Laternfall/), or as a Windows, macOS or Linux download. **37 trophies**, a **speedrun timer**, and save transfer between devices.
- **Comfortable to play**: 3 save slots and auto-save, battle speed up to 4×, repeat-last-turn, gamepad support, rebindable keys, large text and high contrast, a dialogue log
- **Moddable**: game content lives in checked JSON files, with a mod system and an in-browser town editor (see GitHub)
- An original chiptune soundtrack of 21 tracks and a full ending with an epilogue for every hero

### Controls
| | Keyboard | Touch | Gamepad |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Move | Arrow keys / WASD | D-pad | D-pad / stick |
| Confirm / talk / search | Enter, Space or Z | A | A |
| Menu / back | Esc or X | B | B / Start |
| Walk faster, repeat battle turn | Shift | R | X |
| Sound on/off | M | in the menu | Select |

Your progress saves in your browser: three slots plus an auto-save. Save from the menu anywhere, or when you rest at an inn.
